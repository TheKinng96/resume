I led the Ad Gallery from concept through delivery, while the Ad Banner Generator was discontinued after a pilot revealed that it did not fit the team’s workflow. I take responsibility for outcomes, not just code.

My role is not simply to build software, but to solve client problems. By working closely with sales teams and clients, I aim to turn real needs into practical solutions and deliver meaningful business value.
